**Subject:** Your campaign overhead is hiding in plain sight

Campaigns don't fall apart because of bad creative. They fall apart because the systems behind them can't keep up.

If your team is running five or more active channels without automation, you are likely absorbing 20 to 30 percent of project time in coordination overhead alone. That cost never appears on a scope document. It shows up on every margin report.

The shift is from reactive production to proactive strategy. Your team stops reformatting assets and starts making decisions that require human thinking.

Start with an audit of where manual handoffs create the most delay. Then run one campaign through an automated workflow in parallel to validate results without risk.
